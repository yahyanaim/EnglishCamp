## 2. Exam Format Blueprints

### A. IELTS Academic Blueprint
1. **Listening (~30 minutes, 40 questions):**
   - Section 1: Non-academic dialogue (e.g. booking, inquiries). Focus: factual details (dates, names, numbers).
   - Section 2: Non-academic monologue (e.g. guide to a local community center). Focus: location, facilities, schedule.
   - Section 3: Academic dialogue (2–4 participants, e.g. tutor and students discussing coursework). Focus: opinions, agreement/disagreement, research methods.
   - Section 4: Academic lecture monologue. Focus: academic concepts, cause-and-effect, classifications.
   - *Task types:* Form completion, multiple choice, table/flowchart completion, sentence completion, matching.
2. **Reading (60 minutes, 40 questions):**
   - 3 long authentic academic texts (750–950 words each) ranging from descriptive/factual to discursive/analytical.
   - *Task types:* True / False / Not Given, Yes / No / Not Given, Matching Headings, Summary Completion (with word limits), Multiple Choice (4 options).
3. **Writing (60 minutes, 2 tasks):**
   - Task 1 (20 min, min 150 words): Visual data report. Describe, compare, and summarize data presented in a graph, chart, table, or diagram.
   - Task 2 (40 min, min 250 words): Discursive essay responding to a point of view, argument, or problem.
   - *Assessment criteria:* Task Achievement/Response (25%), Coherence & Cohesion (25%), Lexical Resource (25%), Grammatical Range & Accuracy (25%). Task 2 carries 2/3 of total writing score.
4. **Speaking (11–14 minutes, 3 parts):**
   - Part 1 (4–5 min): Familiar personal introduction and questions (hobbies, hometown, studies).
   - Part 2 (3–4 min): Individual long turn. Cue card with 1 min preparation and 1–2 min continuous speaking.
   - Part 3 (4–5 min): Two-way discussion on abstract themes linked to Part 2 topic.

---

### B. IELTS General Training Blueprint
1. **Listening (~30 min, 40 Qs):** Identical to Academic.
2. **Reading (60 minutes, 40 questions):**
   - Section 1: 2–3 short texts on daily survival topics (timetables, notices, advertisements).
   - Section 2: 2 texts related to workplace survival (job descriptions, safety rules, employee handbooks).
   - Section 3: 1 longer text on general interest topic.
3. **Writing (60 minutes, 2 tasks):**
   - Task 1 (20 min, min 150 words): Formal, semi-formal, or personal letter explaining a situation or requesting action.
   - Task 2 (40 min, min 250 words): General interest discursive essay.
4. **Speaking (11–14 min):** Identical to Academic.

---

### C. TOEFL iBT (2026 Redesign) Blueprint
1. **Reading (~30–35 minutes):**
   - Task Family 1: *Complete the Words* (70–100 word academic text with ~10 word endings omitted to assess grammar/morphology).
   - Task Family 2: *Read in Daily Life* (short notices, emails, announcements with 2–3 practical comprehension questions).
   - Task Family 3: *Read an Academic Passage* (condensed academic article with standard multiple choice, vocabulary in context, and inference questions).
2. **Listening (~30–36 minutes):**
   - Task Family 1: *Listen and Choose a Response* (short conversational turns requiring functional conversational selection).
   - Task Family 2: *Listen to an Announcement* (campus announcement with gist/detail questions).
   - Task Family 3: *Listen to a Conversation* (office hours / student services exchange).
   - Task Family 4: *Listen to an Academic Talk* (short lecture with academic comprehension questions).
3. **Writing (~30 minutes):**
   - Task Family 1: *Build a Sentence* (syntax and sentence formation).
   - Task Family 2: *Write an Email* (transactional communicative writing).
   - Task Family 3: *Write for an Academic Discussion* (10 minutes, min 100 words responding to a professor's prompt and contributing original ideas building on classmate posts).
4. **Speaking (~16 minutes):**
   - Task Family 1: *Listen and Repeat* (listen to a spoken sentence and repeat with natural pacing and intonation).
   - Task Family 2: *Take an Interview* (guided conversational questions with prep and response timers).
5. **Scoring:**
   - 1.0 to 6.0 scale in 0.5 increments (A1 to C2). Dual reporting converts to concordant 0–120 scale for university comparisons.

## 3. CEFR Level & Skill Coverage Matrix

| Track / Target Level | CEFR Level | Skill Focus | Target Task Types | Implementation Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation Preparation** | A1–A2 | Vocab & Basic Syntax | Everyday vocabulary, basic notices, short exchanges, personal introductions, simple question formation. | **Active & Enhanced** (Articles, tenses, core vocab) |
| **Intermediate Preparation** | B1 | Connected Text & Gist | Routine conversations, general reading comprehension, basic email writing, expressing preferences and reasons. | **Active & Enhanced** (Reading articles, dialogue completion) |
| **Upper Intermediate (Band 5.5–6.5 / TOEFL 4.0–4.5)** | B2 | Complex Grammar & Argumentation | IELTS GT letters, academic summaries, graph description basics, paragraph development, discussion essays. | **Fully Implemented** (FCE/B2 guide, writing prompts) |
| **Advanced Simulation (Band 7.0–8.0 / TOEFL 5.0–5.5)** | C1 | Nuanced Academic English | IELTS Academic full mock test, TOEFL iBT full simulation, True/False/Not Given, Academic Discussion writing, Part 3 speaking. | **Fully Implemented** (IELTS Acad, IELTS GT, TOEFL iBT) |
| **Mastery & Review (Band 8.5–9.0 / TOEFL 6.0)** | C2 | Precision & Idiomatic Synthesis | Inversion, subtle inference, complex data comparison, high-speed multi-accent listening, advanced collocations. | **Integrated** (AWL advanced, high-band rubrics) |
