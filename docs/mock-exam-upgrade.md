There are **358 unique objective items**, **10 extended-writing prompts** including B2 alternatives, and **22 speaking recording stages** across the distinct content bank. Candidate counts differ because of shared IELTS material and unused TOEFL branches. All objective items include accepted keys, an explanation and an evidence note. Writing models and speaking examples are illustrative; none is labelled as an examiner-awarded score.

## Delivery and assessment

- A wall-clock deadline controls each section, each TOEFL module and each TOEFL writing task. Refreshing does not reset it. On resume, elapsed sections expire using their earlier deadlines rather than granting new time.
- Completed skills/modules are committed. TOEFL reading permits navigation within a module; TOEFL listening moves forward one question at a time. Transcripts and keys are shown after submission.
- IELTS/TOEFL listening has one playback; B2 extracts have two sequential playbacks. Timed speed is 1.0. Review supports replay and slower speeds. Interrupted audio resumes the saved position; a failed load has a retry path.
- The 77 new local neural recordings use the course pipeline and distinct dialogue roles. The owner approved sending these fixed scripts to Microsoft on 30 October. Learner responses were not transmitted. The manifest records text, roles, voices, engine, duration and checksums.
- IELTS map and chart stimuli are original SVGs with text alternatives. B2 speaking uses existing licensed photographs, with original prompts, visible credits and source/licence links. No new image downloads or licence assumptions were needed.
- Real microphone recording replaces the old simulated countdown. Permission is requested before the stimulus. Audio capture follows the prompt and preparation period, stops at the response limit, and is replayable/downloadable. IndexedDB saves each take under an attempt/prompt key. A failed save retains the blob for retry/download and the previous stored take.
- Objective answers use explicit alternatives and word limits. B2 transformations accept 2–5 words including the unchanged key word, expand common negative contractions, and use explicit partial-answer keys. B2 Reading, Use of English and Listening have separate weighted totals (42/28/30).
- Empty answers earn zero. There is **no fabricated writing, speaking, overall score, CEFR classification or 0–120 concordance**. Productive responses are available for qualified assessment. New original items have not been calibrated to an official scale.

## Honest differences from a live examination

1. TOEFL routing uses an openly stated EnglishCamp 70% practice threshold, not ETS's proprietary operational routing or calibration. Fixed 15/15-minute reading modules and 14/15-minute listening modules are local allocations of published base times.
2. TOEFL repetition windows of 8/8/10/10/10/12/12 seconds and 45-second interviews are explicit practice settings. The accessible official paper establishes no preparation period but does not fix per-item windows. These are not presented as verified ETS response clocks.
3. Speaking is a timed rehearsal. A browser does not replace the IELTS examiner or two B2 candidates. B2 interactive communication cannot be established from solo audio. The prompt groups and user-driven transitions differ from a live proctor or automated test sequence.
4. B2 transformation partial credit covers listed fragments; a qualified reviewer can identify other acceptable equivalents. Automatic keys cannot judge every possible natural-language alternative.
5. Natural TTS is not a human performance. Automated word-boundary verification confirms wording, not accent quality, pragmatics or perfect pronunciation. Full listening and independent teacher review remain necessary.
6. There are four first forms, not twelve validated unseen forms. The research plan explicitly expands to three per track **after first-form review and pilots**. Those learner/reviewer steps have not been fabricated.

## Review gate and next bank expansion

Technical validation checks counts, word load, answer variants, partial credit, both adaptive branches, deadlines, restoration, zero-response results, audio files and UI behaviour. These checks do **not** establish equivalent difficulty, score prediction or educational effectiveness.

Before describing the papers as independently reviewed or releasing a 12-form bank:

1. Have a qualified IELTS/TOEFL/B2 reviewer complete each form, challenge the keys and examine distractors and productive rubrics.
2. Listen to every complete audio asset and verify the spoken evidence and pronunciation.
3. Pilot with learners, review omissions, timing, item discrimination and disputed answers, then revise.
4. Author two further genuinely different forms per track, including both TOEFL follow-up branches. Do not count renamed, shuffled or repeatedly shared material as unseen papers.
5. Use actual learner/assessor evidence before introducing any new estimated certification-score mapping.
