# Course media alignment audit

The earlier image section below is retained as an audit record. Current coverage is **100 lessons with 35 internet-sourced assets and 102 inline image placements**; see the [current lesson-image report](lesson-images.md). Video associations and their audit remain unchanged.

Reviewed: 29 October 2026. Project version: 1.0.0.

All 100 current module titles, worked examples and transfer goals were checked against the selected video topics. 26 weaker video associations were replaced. The final selection uses 59 distinct videos from 14 publishers. A shared video supports a specific learning objective; it does not necessarily teach every part of a mixed module. The visible viewing focus and, where needed, coverage note identify that scope.

## Method and limits

- Compared the actual course topic and worked-example reasoning with publisher lesson scripts, transcripts, descriptions and chapter lists. Legacy route IDs were not used to infer exam meaning.
- Checked all selected video IDs, titles, publisher identities and embedding metadata through YouTube oEmbed. Existing source checks dated 28 October and replacement checks dated 28–29 October were retained.
- Verified selected start points against publisher chapters: B2 word formation 7:36; maps 15:19 and process diagrams 18:43; IDP listening strategies 5:50–22:06; IDP reading strategies 9:28–20:46. The BBC agreement excerpt focuses on separated subjects and verbs.
- This is an editorial alignment review, not a claim that every minute of every video was independently watched, that the video is a complete course, or that a teacher's score claim guarantees a result. Videos remain supplementary; the written lesson and activities supply the module-specific practice.
- Current TOEFL guides remain labelled 2026. Earlier Academic Discussion recordings teach a retained task; current course directions define the complete format.
- A process-writing candidate was rejected because its publisher page actually embedded a letter-writing video. A generic vocabulary-notebook video was replaced by topic explanations for hedging and scientific method.

## Images and diagrams

Five sourced assets (two photographs, one scientific graph and two explanatory diagrams) support 8 modules in nine image placements. Each has a local asset, text alternative, source, creator, licence or reuse-policy link, caption, observation question and collapsed explanation. Original image content is unchanged; publisher-provided sizes/renderings and responsive display are recorded in the image manifest. No source page or search-result thumbnail was treated as permission by itself.

- Library photo: develop concrete place description and compare learning resources.
- Bicycle-repair-station photo: describe practical equipment while distinguishing visible evidence from an invented learning story.
- Water cycle: contrast a branching natural cycle with the existing linear juice process.
- Confounding diagram: explain a possible alternative to a causal interpretation of a fictional health comparison.
- Historical NASA graph: practise two-axis reading, yearly versus averaged lines, and observation versus explanation. It is explicitly separate from the fictional cycle-trip data and is not presented as a current-year measurement.

Credits and exact asset checksums are in [the image manifest](../public/media/courses/sourced/manifest.json). The 12 original teaching SVGs are retained.
